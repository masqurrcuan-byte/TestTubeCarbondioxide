Carbon Dioxide Virtual Lab

An interactive virtual lab for Grade 8 science: How do plants and animals affect carbon dioxide concentration?

Students build six test tubes (A to F), predict what will happen, run a simulated 30-minute experiment, record results with hydrogencarbonate indicator colours, and answer guided questions on respiration and photosynthesis.

Part of the BIOLAB series. Based on Cambridge Lower Secondary Science 9 (2nd Edition), Unit 1.3 The Carbon Cycle, pages 28 to 29.
